# dev.6 — ZSS control flow, teams, time attack and music

Status: **feature complete for the dev.6 scope listed below, gates green** (branch `dev.6`, from
`main` after the dev.5 merge, not merged). Last update: 2026-10-05.

dev.5 delivered the single-player game loop. dev.6 adds what the owner asked for next
("the whole game"): the parts of Ikemen GO's ZSS language that were missing, the engine
behaviour behind `ignorehitpause`, a long-standing facing bug, the team modes (Turns and Tag),
Team Arcade / Team Versus / Time Attack on the title menu, and background music the project owns.

## 2. How it was verified

- **EditMode: 263/263.** New: `Dev6EngineTests` (6: for/while/break/continue/MaxLoop, switch,
  ignorehitpause frame by frame, statedef defaults + turning around, kfm_zaxis compiles its loops)
  and `Dev6TeamTests` (7: turns replacement + recovery, tag swap / KO replacement / round end,
  tag recovery, team arcade and team versus setups, time attack flow and time format, every music
  track ships). The 250 dev.5 cases are unchanged.
- **Rendered UI: 175/175** at 1280×720, 2400×1080 and 1024×768. New checks: the three title items,
  the team menu, member picks by taps, a 2-vs-2 tag MatchSetup, both teams in the engine, the TAG
  button visible and swapping the partner, the team line, a fight track playing, and a whole tag
  match ending on the victory screen.
- **Headless harness:** `IK_TEAM=turns|tag tools/harness/h.exe A B` plays CPU team matches;
  `IK_SEED` makes runs reproducible and `IK_STUCK=1` reports a fight frozen for 300 ticks. 48 seeded
  runs (single / turns / tag) finished with no freeze.
- **APK:** `v0.1.0-dev.6` — `com.ayoub.ikmen`, versionName 0.1.0-dev.6, versionCode 6, minSdk 23,
  targetSdk 36, `native-code: arm64-v8a`, IL2CPP, debug-signed (apksigner verify OK);
  40,508,663 bytes, sha256 `4883f7211050507d786e5d3c624808d979228edb705b8ffa699b10542b866fbc`.
- A rendered run at 1024×768 once failed "a fight track plays" because the check read the track
  while the select music was still fading out; `MusicPlayer.Current` (playing or pending) fixed it.
- **Not run:** Firebase Test Lab (no service account) and the owner's Poco F3.

## 3. Known gaps

- **Simul** (2-4 fighters on screen at once) is not implemented: the engine keeps one fighter per
  side on the field. It needs per-side target selection, a 4-player camera and HUD.
- **Tag entry** is instant: the partner appears where the leaving member stood. Ikemen's
  `data/tag.zss` runs the partner in from the screen edge with `TagIn`/`TagOut` states.
- The HUD shows one life bar per side (the member on the field) plus a text line
  "Team n/m"; the motif's team life bars / portraits are not drawn yet.
- The music is generated (chiptune style) — fine for development, replaceable by commissioned
  tracks later (same file names in `Resources/music/`).
- Still open from dev.5: Z axis and 3D stages, exact `HitBy` attribute pairs, `ProjHit = time`
  approximation, the interactive stage's ZSS character, story / netplay / replays / Lua,
  placeholder KFM content and Elecbyte fonts (must be replaced before a sale).
